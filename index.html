Our Story & Flavors

Where the Spice of the Levant Meets the Fire of Mexico

At Yalla Habibi, we believe great food shouldn't be boring or complicated. Born in the heart of Lucena City, we combine the slow-roasted, aromatic warmth of Mediterranean shawarma with the bold, zesty spirit of authentic Mexican street food. Whether you are craving a high-protein post-workout bowl or sharing loaded fries with your crew, we’ve got your table ready!
